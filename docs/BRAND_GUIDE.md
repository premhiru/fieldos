## Brand Idea

Caladrona is operational intelligence for the physical world. Its identity communicates attentive oversight, grounded evidence, and decisive follow-through. The brand should feel appropriate in an airport operations center, infrastructure program office, or field coordination room.

## Identity System

The Caladrona mark is a compact rounded square that moves from amber to teal. Amber represents conditions that require attention; teal represents a known, healthy operational state. The transition expresses Caladrona's role in turning field signals into coordinated action.

- **Primary lockup:** mark, `Caladrona` wordmark, and optional `Operational intelligence` descriptor.
- **Compact lockup:** mark and wordmark for mobile headers.
- **App icon and favicon:** standalone gradient mark.
- **Loading mark:** the mark beside neutral skeleton structures; do not spin or distort the logo.
- **Clear space:** preserve at least one quarter of the mark width on all sides.
- **Minimum size:** 24 px digital. Use the simplified standalone mark below 32 px.

## Typography

Caladrona uses Instrument Sans, with the platform sans-serif stack as a resilient fallback. JetBrains Mono is reserved for compact dates, identifiers, and operational metadata. Weights are limited to regular, medium, semibold, and bold. Display typography is reserved for authentication and true first-impression moments; operational screens use compact headings.

## Color

Warm white and graphite carry the product. Amber and teal establish identity while retaining clear operational meaning; blue remains informational rather than becoming the dominant brand color.

| Role     | Use                                 | Reference |
| -------- | ----------------------------------- | --------- |
| Graphite | Primary actions and text            | `#15130d` |
| Teal     | Healthy, connected, complete        | `#0f9d86` |
| Amber    | Attention, review, brand transition | `#ff7a33` |
| Red      | Critical, destructive, failed       | `#ce3e45` |
| Blue     | Informational and linked evidence   | `#357391` |
| Canvas   | Warm operational workspace          | `#f6f4ee` |

Color must always be paired with text, an icon, or a structural cue when it communicates status.

## Illustration

Empty-state illustrations use thin operational frames, one familiar Lucide icon, neutral borders, and no decorative scenes. They should suggest an organized workspace waiting for data. Do not use mascots, stock photography, AI motifs, gradients, or abstract decoration.

## Voice

Caladrona is direct, calm, and useful. State what happened, why it matters, and what the user can do. Prefer `No conversations found` over system implementation language. Avoid hype, excessive reassurance, and anthropomorphizing AI.

## Usage Rules

- Do not replace or reorder the amber-to-teal mark colors.
- Do not stretch, rotate, outline, or place the mark in another container.
- Do not use blue as a dominant background or decorative accent.
- Do not pair the wordmark with a second display typeface.
- Do not use teal or amber as broad decorative fills; both retain operational meaning.
